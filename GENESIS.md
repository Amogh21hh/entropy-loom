## Round 2 — The Cynic's Execution

- **#1 Ricci Choir** — killed. Discrete Ricci-flow demos exist; curvature-as-pitch is a mapping, not a mechanism.
- **#2 Szilard Exchange** — killed. The "market" would be hand-tuned heuristics; Landauer accounting is just a counter.
- **#3 Barcode Organ** — killed. TDA sonification already appears in papers, and barcodes of hand-drawn clouds get dull fast.
- **#4 Kirchhoff Harp** — killed. Graph-spectrum sonification exists, and it sounds like a bad xylophone.
- **#5 Tropical Terrarium** — killed. Pretty visuals with no interaction loop that teaches you anything.
- **#6 Sandpile Notary** — killed. Sandpile fractals are everywhere, and the cryptography framing is not secure. It's a gimmick.
- **#7 Kolmogorov Garden** — killed. Kolmogorov complexity can't be computed, so any "compressor" is a fake proxy.
- **#8 Proof Weather** — killed. It's a metaphor with no real dynamics. You could hard-code it with templates.
- **#9 Hamiltonian Ink** — killed. It's a teaching demo of integrator drift. You'd play with it for minutes, not hours.
- **#10 Entropy Loom** — **survives.** Braidlab (MATLAB) and taffy-puller papers do exist, but they are offline analysis tools. No interactive instrument links a live, exactly area-preserving stirring flow to an exact integer computation of its mapping class and entropy, along with the curve's tightened minimal position and its sound. The core can't be faked. Every number is either exact (BigInt max-plus arithmetic, integer characteristic polynomials) or has a provable bound (Burau ≤ λ, geometric ≥ minimal intersection). The fluid stretches by the same exponential rate that the integer engine predicts, and you can watch the two agree.
