    Playing the loom

    • Click above a gap to swap two rods counter-clockwise (σᵢ). Click below it for clockwise (σᵢ⁻¹).
    • Drag a rod over or under its neighbour to swap them.
    • 1–8 play σᵢ, and Shift+1–8 play σᵢ⁻¹. Space loops the protocol, Backspace undoes the last swap, R reverses the whole word, G shows the tightened curve, M toggles sound and C re-seeds the dye.
    • You can type protocols as 1 -2, σ1 σ2⁻¹ or aB (capital letters mean inverses).

    The fluid is stretched by an exactly area-preserving twist flow. Alongside it, integer arithmetic on Dynnikov coordinates tracks the least stretching that any curve with the same topology could have. The dye can never beat that minimum, and its growth rate converges to λ.
